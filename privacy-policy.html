Privacy Policy

How we handle and protect information you share with ExamSupportHub.

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Information you share with us

When you ask about or use our services, you may share contact details, messages, study materials, or other information needed to understand your request and provide the agreed support. This website does not currently have an account system or an on-page contact form. If you contact us through WhatsApp, WhatsApp processes your phone number and messages under its own terms and privacy policy.

Confidentiality and use of client information

We treat client messages and materials as confidential and limit access to people who need the information to respond to you, provide agreed services, or handle support. We do not sell or rent clients' personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We may use service providers to help operate our services, respond to a request, or process a transaction; where we do, information is shared only as needed for that purpose. We may also disclose information if required by law or necessary to protect someone's rights or safety.

Website and service providers

Our hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser, and requested page to deliver and protect this website. The site also loads a Tawk.to chat service, and WhatsApp links open a service operated by WhatsApp. These providers may process information under their own privacy notices and terms. We do not control their systems or privacy practices.

Data protection and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information we handle, but no online service or method of transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, provide requested services, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card details, or other highly sensitive information in a chat.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to contact us through third-party services. You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you have shared with us, subject to records we must retain by law or for legitimate business purposes.

Changes and questions

We may update this policy as our services or practices change. For questions or privacy requests, contact ExamSupportHub through the Contact Us page.
